# P003 M8 — NC-01 Urea Negative-Control Adversarial Pass

## Decision
**NC-01 survives the direct-Russia-urea exposure attack and is promoted to PROVISIONAL NEGATIVE CONTROL (R2), subject to final cutoff/vintage and indirect-channel checks.**

## Target
2022 Russia/Ukraine fertilizer shock → material Nigeria-specific warning based on **direct Russian urea exposure (HS 310210)**.

The target is deliberately narrower than "fertilizer" or "global urea prices".

## Reporter-side evidence
Nigeria's 2021 reporter record:
- world urea imports: USD 161.82k;
- Netherlands: USD 157.91k;
- India: USD 2.95k;
- Turkey: USD 0.96k;
- no Russia entry in Nigeria's displayed partner record.

## Mirror-side attack
Exporter-to-Nigeria mirror data do reveal a Russian shipment that Nigeria's reporter table does not display:
- Russia → Nigeria urea: USD 8.85k, 27,785 kg.

Therefore the earlier wording "no Russian urea exposure" would be false if interpreted literally.

However, the mirror amount is extremely small relative to:
- Russia's global 2021 urea exports: about USD 2.490bn / 7.006bn kg;
- Nigeria's own 2021 urea exports: about USD 906.350m / 1.331bn kg;
- mirror-reported Iran → Nigeria urea: about USD 121.710m / 259.789m kg.

The bilateral Russia→Nigeria urea channel was therefore **non-zero but negligible in scale** in these reconstructed 2021 records.

## Important asymmetry discovery
Nigeria reporter imports (USD 161.82k) and partner mirror exports to Nigeria (notably Iran USD 121.710m) disagree radically.

This means:
1. Nigerian urea import data have a serious reporter/mirror asymmetry;
2. absence in reporter data cannot be treated as absence of imports;
3. the control must rely on bilateral Russia-specific mirror evidence, not Nigeria's total reporter imports alone;
4. indirect/re-export channels remain a separate unresolved question.

## Domestic-position context
WITS reports Nigeria exported about USD 906.350m / 1.331bn kg of urea in 2021. Nigeria was therefore a major urea exporter in the same pre-event year.

This strengthens the case against a narrative of direct Nigerian dependence on Russian urea, but it does not prove insulation from global urea/fertilizer prices or gas/input shocks.

## What NC-01 can and cannot test
Can test:
"Did a baseline incorrectly convert the Russia fertilizer shock into a material Nigeria warning on the basis of direct Russian urea import dependence?"

Cannot test:
"Was Nigeria economically unaffected by the global fertilizer shock?"
"Could global urea prices, natural-gas channels, regional trade, or other fertilizer products affect Nigeria?"

## Current state
T1 global event: separately anchorable.
T2 direct Russia→Nigeria HS310210 exposure: **NEGLIGIBLE, not zero**, in retrospective 2021 mirror data.
Downstream warning based specifically on direct Russian-urea dependence: expected to be withheld.

Replay mode: R2 until publication/vintage availability at the chosen event cutoff is demonstrated.
