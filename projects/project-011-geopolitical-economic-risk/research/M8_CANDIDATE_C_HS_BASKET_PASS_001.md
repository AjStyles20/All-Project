# P003 M8 — Candidate C Vegetable-Oil HS Basket Pass 001

## Status
**MAJOR SCOPE CORRECTION — refined-vs-crude legal treatment must be separated before T2.**

## Official classification anchor
Nigeria's CET/Customs Chapter 15 identifies relevant vegetable-oil headings including:
- 1507 soya-bean oil;
- 1508 ground-nut oil;
- 1511 palm oil;
- 1512 sunflower/safflower/cotton-seed oil;
- 1513 coconut/palm-kernel/babassu oil;
- 1514 rape/colza/mustard oil;
- 1515 other fixed vegetable oils.

The exact basket must be frozen at tariff-line level, not merely "HS Chapter 15".

## Critical legal-treatment discovery
Nigeria's Trade Information Portal lists **refined vegetable oils and fats** under the import-prohibition list, while explicitly stating that **crude vegetable oil is not banned from importation**.

Therefore the October 2023 CBN FX-access category "Vegetable oils" cannot be operationalized as though all refined and crude vegetable-oil tariff lines were equally importable.

This creates a required distinction:
1. FX-access eligibility/restriction;
2. customs/import prohibition;
3. tariff classification;
4. actual import exposure.

Removing an official-FX restriction does not itself legalize a product that remains prohibited from importation.

## Consequence for Candidate C
The earlier broad consumer-retail chain:
FX access removal → imported retail vegetable oil → NBS bottled vegetable-oil price
is **not admissible without line-level legal and product mapping**.

A more defensible candidate may focus on crude vegetable-oil inputs that are legally importable and then test a processing/downstream channel, but that requires evidence of:
- exact crude HS lines;
- Nigerian imports;
- domestic refining/processing linkage;
- mapping to the retail outcome.

## Provisional basket architecture
Do not aggregate yet.

A. crude lines — potentially admissible, subject to exact tariff/legal verification;
B. refined/other lines — screen against prohibition and exemptions before inclusion;
C. prepared mixtures/margarine — separate products, not silently pooled.
