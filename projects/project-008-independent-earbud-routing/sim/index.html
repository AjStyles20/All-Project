Earbud Routing Lab

A simulation of per-ear routing for a pair of earbuds. Wear ordinary headphones: the left and right channels stand in for the left and right buds. All sounds are generated in the page.

Simulation only. Real phones don't let ordinary apps route other apps' audio or calls per ear, and per-ear sources over LE Audio are patent-encumbered (see the README).
