## Reviewer instructions
You are evaluating whether each atomic claim is justified by the evidence packet under the declared replay mode and information boundary. You are not evaluating writing quality and you are not being asked whether the claim sounds plausible.

For each claim select exactly one:
- SUPPORTED
- PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- UNSUPPORTED
- CONTRADICTED
- INDETERMINATE

Then provide a short evidence-based rationale.

If the item is an abstention/withheld transition, select:
- APPROPRIATE_ABSTENTION
- OVER_ABSTENTION
- INDETERMINATE_ABSTENTION

## Administration rules
1. Baseline identity is hidden.
2. Claim order is randomized outside this template.
3. Identical claims use identical evidence packets.
4. Reviewers work independently before discussion.
5. Original responses are immutable after submission.
6. Adjudication, if needed, is stored separately.
7. No simulated/fabricated reviewer response may be entered as evidence.
