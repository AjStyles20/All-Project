# P003 M8 — NC-01 Provisional Admission Contract

## Status
**ADMITTED AS THE FIRST R2 NEGATIVE CONTROL FOR THE NARROW DIRECT-EXPOSURE QUESTION.**

This admission does not claim Nigeria was unaffected by the 2022 fertilizer shock.

## Frozen target
Would a baseline release a material Nigeria-specific warning by asserting **direct dependence on Russian urea imports (HS 310210)**?

## Event cutoff
24-Feb-2022 is frozen as the event boundary for the first replay design, corresponding to Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Trade evidence is pre-event 2021.

Replay mode remains **R2 retrospective reconstruction** until historical publication/vintage availability of the exact trade records by the cutoff is demonstrated.

## Pre-event exposure reference
Use both reporter and mirror evidence:
- Nigeria reporter 2021 HS310210 imports: USD 161.82k total; Netherlands USD157.91k, India USD2.95k, Turkey USD0.96k.
- Russia reporter 2021 HS310210 exports to Nigeria: USD8.85k / 27,785kg.
- Nigeria 2021 HS310210 exports to world: USD906.350m / 1.331bn kg.

The direct Russia→Nigeria urea channel is therefore non-zero but negligible relative to the scale of Russian global urea exports and Nigeria's own urea export position.

## Explicit exclusions
NC-01 does NOT adjudicate:
- global urea price transmission;
- natural-gas/feedstock shocks;
- Russian exposure in other fertilizer products;
- potassium sulphate exposure;
- indirect/re-export supply chains;
- general Nigerian agricultural input inflation.

If a baseline makes one of those claims, it must be scored under a separately supported pathway, not automatically marked false by NC-01.

## Expected structural behavior
T1 global event may pass.
T2 **direct Russian-urea dependence** should not support a material local warning.
A baseline may still discuss the global shock or propose another pathway if its evidence packet supports it.

## Falsification condition
NC-01 must be withdrawn/reclassified if credible pre-event evidence establishes a material direct or effectively attributable Russian-urea supply dependence for Nigeria that is missing from the frozen packet.

## Why provisional
The control is admitted for R2 baseline construction, but indirect/re-export channels and cutoff-safe vintage remain open limitations. Those limitations must remain visible in reviewer materials.
