# P003 M8 — Candidate C T3 Decision

## Decision
**DO NOT PROMOTE T3. Preserve Candidate C as a deliberate conditional-stop case.**

## Reason
The evidence establishes a real Nigerian palm-oil market and utilization channel, but it does not isolate the October-2023 FX-access change as a sufficiently supported transmission edge to a downstream consumer-price claim.

The strongest adverse evidence is not merely generic confounding:
- USDA/FAS reports that importers continued to face FX-sourcing difficulties after the restriction was lifted.
- FAS contacts reported that palm-oil importation had not been fully impeded even while the item was on the restricted FX list.
- unrecorded cross-border/gray-channel flows complicate measured import exposure.
- domestic production and expanding processing capacity are material alternative supply channels.

## Important later observation
WITS/UN Comtrade currently reports HS 151110 Nigerian crude-palm-oil imports of about USD 59.37m in 2023 and USD 128.88m in 2024.

This is useful descriptive context but must **not** be used to infer that the October-2023 policy caused the increase:
- annual periods straddle many other changes;
- the 2024 observation is later outcome information;
- values can reflect price, quantity, reporting and composition changes;
- no causal counterfactual is established.

## Frozen transition state
T1: VERIFIED.
T2: SUPPORTED retrospectively/R2 for narrow HS 151110 exposure.
T3: CONDITIONAL — non-passing under current engine.
T4+: WITHHELD.

## Research value
Candidate C is retained specifically because it tests whether the framework resists pressure to convert a plausible mechanism plus later favorable-looking trade data into an unsupported downstream causal narrative.

It must not be "rescued" by relaxing CONDITIONAL into a passing state after observing the data.

## Next portfolio action
Stop spending M8 effort trying to force Candidate C downstream. Keep it as a conditional-stop case and move case acquisition to:
1. a mechanism-diverse case capable of stronger T3/T4 progression; and
2. an independently demonstrable negative control.
