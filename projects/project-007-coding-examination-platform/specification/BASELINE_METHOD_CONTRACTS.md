# P001 Baseline and Method Contracts — v1.0 Development Specification

## B0 — Final submission baseline
Permitted evidence: final submission plus ordinary tests/rubric.
No process evidence, fixed viva, competence-model targeting or gap-driven verification.

## B1 — Process-evidence baseline
B0 plus controlled examination process/event evidence.
No targeted verification.

## B2 — Generic fixed-viva baseline
B1 plus the same pre-frozen generic viva/code-explanation procedure for eligible cases.
The viva is not selected from an evidence gap.

## B3 — Evidence-centered competence model
Uses the structured competence/evidence model to form bounded claim states.
No targeted follow-up verification is selected from detected gaps.

## B4 — EGPCV method
Uses the evidence-centered competence model, detects explicit evidence gaps, filters candidate probes for admissibility and potential sufficiency, then selects the lowest-burden candidate among adequate probes. Probe results become additional evidence and may update claim state.

## Cross-method rule

The same underlying case is used across eligible B0–B4 comparisons. Methods differ by evidence/mechanism access rather than by silently changing the case.

## Valid outcomes

B4 may outperform simpler methods, tie them, add unnecessary burden, or perform worse. B3/B2/B1 sufficiency is a valid scientific result. No software test establishes comparative research superiority.
