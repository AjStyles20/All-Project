# Project 005 — Intelligent Online Chess Platform

## Concept
An online chess environment combining ordinary chess play with selected communication and intelligent-support capabilities. Candidate directions include voice/video interaction, AI-assisted rule/arbitration support, session management, and explicitly separated no-assistance versus assistance-permitted modes.

## Current stage
RESEARCH / DIFFERENTIATION FOUNDATION.

## Research mandate
Map chess-platform history and current products, computer chess/engines, online matchmaking, communication features, arbitration/rule support, fair-play and anti-cheating systems, rated/unrated assistance policies, relevant open-source platforms, accessibility, moderation/privacy/security, and the exact user problem not adequately addressed by established platforms.

## Boundary
Do not claim novelty merely by combining familiar chess-platform features. Differentiation must survive direct comparison with established systems and projects.
