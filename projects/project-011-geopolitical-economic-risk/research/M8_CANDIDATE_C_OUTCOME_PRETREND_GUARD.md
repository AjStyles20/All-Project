## Consequence
Any later increase cannot be scored as evidence that the FX-access change caused prices to rise.
Any later decline cannot by itself establish that the policy caused prices to fall.

The event occurs inside a broader FX-regime change: CBN records market unification beginning 14-Jun-2023 and later accessibility of FX for the 43 previously restricted items.
