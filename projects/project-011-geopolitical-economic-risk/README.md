# P011 — Geopolitical Risk to Economic Impact Forecasting and Warning System

## Class
GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

## Working problem
Monitor national and international political events and estimate plausible downstream economic effects for a selected country, market, sector, or household context.

## Research before implementation
Research must cover geopolitical-risk measurement, event extraction, economic transmission channels, causal inference versus correlation, scenario analysis, forecasting, country-specific exposure, uncertainty communication, misinformation/data quality, commercial intelligence systems, open-source prior art, and evaluation against historical outcomes.

## Current status
NOT STARTED — high-priority portfolio research candidate after P007.

## Guardrail
The system must not present speculative causal chains as facts. Forecasts must carry uncertainty, evidence provenance, time horizon, and a distinction between observed events, inferred transmission mechanisms, and predicted effects.
