# P001 M8 — Independent Assessor Administration Guide v1

Status: PRE-ASSESSOR READY instrument; administration guide only. No assessor judgment is recorded by this file.

## Who may assess

Use a person with suitable Computer Science/programming or programming-assessment knowledge who can understand a small Python function and test-design evidence. Prefer two assessors where feasible. They must judge independently.

## What the researcher gives the assessor

Give only the blinded assessor dry-run pack and Sections A-G of the independent assessor worksheet. Do not provide construction expectations, hidden case notes, B0-B4 system states, B4 selection output, researcher-only Section H, or another assessor's answer.

## Administration order

1. Assign a non-identifying assessor code such as ASSESSOR-A. Do not encode unnecessary personal information in the research record.
2. State that this is a dry run of the assessment rubric, not an exam of the assessor.
3. Provide the frozen CC3 state definitions.
4. Present CASE-PILOT-001 and collect the complete judgment before discussing it.
5. Present CASE-PILOT-003 and collect the complete judgment before discussing it.
6. Ask the assessor to confirm the independence declaration.
7. Only after submission may the researcher reveal construction notes/system outputs for methodological discussion.
8. Preserve the original judgment even if post-submission discussion changes the assessor's opinion. Any later revision is a new version/record, not an overwrite.

## Required fields per case

Record evidence state; three CC3 dimensions; rationale; boundary confidence/ambiguity; additional evidence needed; human-review concern; independence confirmation.

## Two-assessor rule

Assessor B must not see Assessor A's worksheet, comments, state, or post-judgment discussion before submitting B's own judgments. Do not ask B to resolve A. Disagreement is data about rubric clarity.

## Prohibited researcher behavior

Do not tell the assessor that CASE-PILOT-001 is expected to be UNRESOLVED or CASE-PILOT-003 is expected to be PARTIAL. Do not explain that the expected output in CASE-PILOT-003 is wrong before judgment. Do not say B4 is the proposed contribution or that a particular result would help the project. Do not coach the assessor toward agreement.

## After both judgments

Record the judgments without alteration, compare raw agreement/disagreement, catalogue ambiguous boundaries and requested additional evidence, and decide whether the rubric/instrument needs a versioned revision and another dry run. Do not proceed to M9 Method Freeze merely because assessors agree; review whether agreement is based on the intended rule and whether the cases exposed the planned boundaries.
