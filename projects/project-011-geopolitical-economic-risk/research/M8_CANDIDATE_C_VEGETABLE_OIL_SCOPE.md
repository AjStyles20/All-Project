# P003 M8 — Candidate C Product-Scope Decision

## Decision
**Freeze vegetable oils as the first Candidate C product family for deeper screening.**

This is a screening decision, not evidence that the October 2023 FX-access policy caused a price or import outcome.

## Why vegetable oils
- explicitly included among the 43 categories whose official FX access restriction was lifted;
- consumer-price observations exist in NBS Selected Food Prices material;
- a plausible import/retail transmission chain can be stated and then attacked;
- unlike a purely industrial category, the downstream consumer target is observable.

## Why rice is not first
Rice is also explicitly included, but domestic-production policy, border/import restrictions, local supply and politically salient rice interventions create a particularly dense policy-confounding environment. It remains a reserve product, not rejected.

## Frozen chain to test
T1: 12-Oct-2023 FX-access restriction removal
→ T2: vegetable-oil import exposure / eligibility
→ T3: official-FX-access and landed-cost/supply transmission
→ T4: conditional vegetable-oil retail-price/import-availability scenario.

No T5/T6 without an independently specified and validated quantitative model.

## Required evidence before T2/T3 release
1. exact HS basket for vegetable oils, predeclared and versioned;
2. Nigeria import exposure before the policy under consistent reporter/flow/period definitions;
3. proof that the category mapping corresponds to the policy category;
4. contemporaneous FX regime/liquidity evidence;
5. independent domestic retail-price observations;
6. treatment of tariffs, import prohibitions, local production, inventories/contracts and exchange-rate movements.

## Outcome discipline
Pre-policy NBS food-price data already show vegetable-oil prices were moving before October 2023. Therefore post-policy price movement cannot be treated as a clean policy effect.

The evaluation target must be frozen before post-policy outcome scoring and must distinguish:
- directional consistency;
- causal attribution;
- numerical forecast eligibility.

## Current state
T1: VERIFIED.
T2: HOLD pending frozen HS/import-exposure reconstruction.
T3+: WITHHELD.
