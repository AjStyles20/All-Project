# Project 009 — Interactive AI Virtual Audience for Presentation and Public-Speaking Rehearsal

## Concept
A simulated audience environment for practicing speeches, presentations, pitches, lectures, interviews, and related live communication. Candidate audience personas may ask questions, challenge claims, represent different expertise levels or attitudes, and provide bounded feedback.

## Current stage
RESEARCH FOUNDATION — relationship to Project 001 is unresolved.

## Research mandate
Study public-speaking/presentation training lineage, virtual audiences and exposure/rehearsal systems, conversational agents, VR/avatars where relevant, AI presentation/interview products, audience simulation research, feedback validity, accessibility, privacy, and evidence for transfer from simulation to real performance. Directly compare the concept with Project 001 and test whether a separate product/problem remains defensible.

## Boundary
