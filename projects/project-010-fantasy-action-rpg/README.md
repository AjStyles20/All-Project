# Project 010 — Original Party-Based Fantasy Action RPG

## Concept
An original game concept inspired at a high level by the appeal of ensemble fantasy adventures: a party of distinctive characters, complementary abilities, fantasy combat, exploration, narrative progression, and team dynamics.

## Current stage
CONCEPT / RESEARCH FOUNDATION.

## Research mandate
Verify the game and licensing landscape surrounding the referenced inspiration, identify any official or announced related games, examine adjacent party-based fantasy/action RPGs and co-op systems, identify gameplay/market gaps only where evidence supports them, study feasible engines/tooling and scope for available hardware/time, and develop an original gameplay/narrative identity.

## IP boundary
Do not copy protected characters, names, dialogue, storylines, locations, artwork, music, or other protected expressive elements. Inspiration is not evidence of permission. No claim that a game “has never been done” until the relevant landscape has been searched and bounded.
