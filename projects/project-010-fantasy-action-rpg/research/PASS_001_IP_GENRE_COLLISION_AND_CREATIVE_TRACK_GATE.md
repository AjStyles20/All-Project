# P014 — Fantasy Action RPG
## Pass 001 — IP, Genre Collision and Creative-Track Gate

### Canonical clarification
AJ clarified that the project is essentially a game inspired by *The Legend of Vox Machina*. This should not be interpreted as a literal licensed adaptation unless permission is obtained from the rights holder.

### Research position
The project is a creative/entertainment build rather than a strong research-novelty candidate. Party-based fantasy RPGs with distinct combat roles, companion banter, branching choices, reactive relationships, tactical combat and ensemble storytelling are mature design territory. A game can still be excellent without claiming novelty in those elements.

### IP boundary
Critical Role's public content policy identifies Vox Machina campaign names, characters, attributes/appearances, story plots, locations, dialogue/interactions and related material as protected IP. Licensed Vox Machina games already exist. Therefore a public or commercial project should not copy Vox Machina names, characters, Exandria locations, story arcs, visual identity or other protected expression without authorization.

### Safe creative reframing
Use *The Legend of Vox Machina* only as inspiration for high-level qualities such as:
- a dysfunctional but likeable adventuring party;
- mature humour and emotional stakes;
- distinct combat roles and powers;
- party banter and interpersonal conflict;
- episodic quests that build toward larger threats;
- a balance of spectacle, comedy and character drama.

Create an original world, lore, factions, characters, names, visual design, monsters, quests, dialogue and narrative arcs.

### Novelty assessment
Not suitable for the research-novelty shortlist unless a separate, falsifiable game-AI/HCI/systems research question is introduced independently of the Vox-Machina-inspired creative premise. Forcing novelty through ordinary RPG mechanics would be manufactured novelty.

### Engineering/portfolio value
Potentially strong as a creative portfolio project if scoped tightly. A realistic student prototype could focus on one playable region, a small party, 2–4 combat classes, one major quest chain, companion interactions, and a compact combat loop rather than attempting a full AAA-style RPG.

### Decision
**PARK AS CREATIVE/ENTERTAINMENT PROJECT. REMOVE FROM RESEARCH-NOVELTY/FYP SHORTLIST.**

The idea remains viable as an original fantasy action RPG inspired by ensemble-adventure tone, but not as a literal Vox Machina adaptation and not as a novelty-driven research project in its current form.
