# P003 M8 — Candidate A T4 Scenario Contract

## Status
**T4 CLAIM FORM FROZEN; OUTCOME NOT SCORED. Candidate remains R2 retrospective reconstruction.**

## Mechanism-aligned scenario

Given:
1. an authoritatively verified Russia–Ukraine disruption;
2. retrospectively established Nigerian import exposure to affected wheat origins;
3. an independently documented Nigerian wheat→milling→flour/bread pathway;
4. documented contemporaneous global wheat-price pressure;
5. explicit competing channels including foreign-exchange scarcity, freight/energy costs, supplier substitution, domestic supply and policy;

the strongest admissible T4 statement is:

> **The disruption creates upward pressure on Nigerian wheat-flour and bread/cereal costs over the subsequent months, conditional on import exposure not being sufficiently offset by supplier substitution, exchange-rate developments, inventories/contracts, domestic supply, logistics conditions or policy intervention.**

This is a directional scenario, not a causal attribution, point forecast, probability, or numerical magnitude estimate.

## Forbidden stronger statements at T4
- “The war caused Nigerian inflation.”
- “Bread and cereals will rise by X%.”
- “Nigeria will experience X% food inflation because of the event.”
- any calibrated probability;
- any claim that all movement in the Bread-and-cereals CPI category is attributable to wheat or to the event.

Those require stronger identification and/or T5/T6 model eligibility.

## Horizon contract

Use a **broad 3–6 month directional evaluation window** after the event anchor for the first R2 scenario assessment.

Reason for freezing a window rather than a single optimized month:
- physical import, milling, inventory and retail-price transmission is not instantaneous;
- supplier substitution and contracts can delay or attenuate transmission;
- selecting the best-looking post-event month after seeing outcomes would create outcome-optimization bias.

This window is a methodological design choice and must be sensitivity-tested later. It is not asserted as an empirically estimated Nigerian pass-through lag.

## Evaluation target

Primary mechanism-aligned outcome family:
- NBS Bread and cereals / closest historically consistent pre-rebasing CPI class or underlying price series that can be reproducibly recovered.

Supporting, not substituting, observations:
- NBS/USDA-reported wheat flour and bread prices;
- international wheat-price context;
- exchange-rate/freight/energy context.

The exact historical series and transformation must be frozen before numerical scoring.

If counterevidence is material but unresolved, T4 must be CONDITIONAL or CONTESTED and the current conservative progression engine will not release the scenario as passed.
