# AJ Project Portfolio — Project Catalogue

## Purpose
This is the canonical short-form registry of current AJ Project OS project ideas. It records what each project means before portfolio-wide research, prior-art analysis, feasibility review, and implementation. Titles are working titles and may change only through an explicit project decision.

## Portfolio rule
No project is to be called unique, first-of-its-kind, unprecedented, secure, production-ready, or otherwise stronger than the available evidence supports. Research must distinguish VERIFIED FACT, REASONED INFERENCE, UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS, and UNKNOWN. Absence from search results is not evidence of non-existence.

## Portfolio classes
- **GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM** — intended to solve a problem for a broad class of users, organizations, schools, businesses, or markets.
- **PERSONAL-FIRST SYSTEM** — originates from AJ's own need or curiosity but may later generalize to other users.
- **PERSONAL MASTERY PROJECT** — valuable because AJ wants to understand and build it personally; external adoption or novelty is not required for the project to remain worthwhile.
- **CREATIVE / ENTERTAINMENT PROJECT** — primarily creative engineering, design, or entertainment rather than institutional/problem-solving software.

Project class does not determine research priority by itself. Portfolio prioritization considers opportunity, feasibility, evidence strength, risk, implementation cost, and strategic purpose separately.

### Project 001 — Source-Grounded AI Presentation, Interview and Defense Simulator
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

An AI-assisted rehearsal environment in which users provide their own documents, reports, slides, research materials, or other sources and face configurable virtual reviewers. It generates evidence-grounded questions, accepts typed or spoken answers, conducts bounded follow-up questioning, and returns explainable qualitative feedback tied to authoritative source evidence. Potential contexts include academic defenses, presentations, interviews, oral examinations, and professional reviews.

**Status:** PAUSED — implemented prototype under verification. Do not restart from scratch.

### Project 002 — AI-Powered Virtual Classroom and Personal Learning World
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM, with a PERSONAL-FIRST origin condition

A virtual learning environment that must remain useful even for one human learner. Users provide learning materials and interact with configurable AI teachers, tutors, peers, skeptics, mentors, examiners, or other bounded roles. Research has narrowed the strongest contribution candidates to authority-preserving educational knowledge dependencies and assistance-to-independence mastery verification, with human/AI role contracts as supporting architecture.

**Status:** CONDITIONAL GO / PARKED FOR PORTFOLIO COMPARISON. Research mature enough to pause; implementation intentionally not started.

### Project 003 — AJ's Educational Language Model From Scratch
**Class:** PERSONAL MASTERY PROJECT

AJ wants to design, train, evaluate, and operate his own language model from random initialization rather than merely wrapping or fine-tuning a commercial model. The project should expose and make understandable the complete pipeline: corpus design, tokenization, embeddings, attention, transformer blocks, optimization, checkpoints, inference, evaluation, computational limits, and failure modes. Research contributions such as curriculum or misconception-aware training may be pursued where evidence supports them, but novelty is not required to justify building the project.

**Primary success condition:** AJ can truthfully say he designed and trained his own functioning language model from scratch and can explain the major components and tradeoffs.

**Status:** ACTIVE PERSONAL RESEARCH / BUILD-REGARDLESS-OF-NOVELTY TRACK.

### Project 004 — Gamified School Achievement, Rewards and Digital Economy System
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

A school-oriented platform that converts selected academic, extracurricular, behavioral, or institutional achievements into a governed points/reward economy. Students could earn, track, and potentially spend or exchange points under institutional rules. Research must address motivation, fairness, incentive design, abuse/fraud resistance, accessibility, governance, privacy, and whether a digital economy adds real educational value rather than superficial gamification.

**Status:** CONCEPT STAGE; PROBLEM AND INCENTIVE MODEL REQUIRE RESEARCH.

### Project 005 — Intelligent Online Chess Platform
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

An online chess environment combining normal chess play with selected communication and intelligent-support capabilities. Candidate directions include voice/video interaction, AI-assisted rule/arbitration support, session management, and clearly separated modes in which outside assistance is either prohibited or explicitly allowed. Existing chess platforms, engines, fair-play systems, communication features, and anti-cheating approaches must be reviewed before differentiation is claimed.

**Status:** RESEARCH AND DIFFERENTIATION REQUIRED.

### Project 006 — Personal Screen Memory System
**Class:** PERSONAL-FIRST SYSTEM

A user-controlled system intended to help people recover information they previously saw or worked with on a computer. Candidate functions include selective screen/activity capture, chronological organization, OCR or other extraction, activity segmentation, semantic/lexical search, and questions such as “Where did I see this?” Privacy, consent, storage, encryption, retention, security, indexing cost, OS integration, and existing activity-recall systems are central to feasibility.

**Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED; PRIVACY AND SECURITY ARE FIRST-CLASS CONSTRAINTS.

### Project 007 — Intelligent Coding Examination Platform
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM
**Origin:** Personal observed problem — programming knowledge being assessed by handwriting code on paper in school tests/examinations.

A purpose-built programming examination environment with an integrated editor/execution workflow and configurable examination controls. Candidate evidence may include execution history, focus/tab events, clipboard events, and other bounded integrity indicators. The system should not automatically label a student a cheater merely because an event occurs; it should preserve contextual evidence for authorized human review. Research must cover online judges, computer-based testing, plagiarism/integrity systems, lockdown/proctoring approaches, AI-era assessment, accessibility, privacy, security, and false-positive risk.

**Status:** STRONG GENERAL/FYP CANDIDATE; PRIOR-ART, SECURITY, ASSESSMENT, AND ETHICS RESEARCH REQUIRED.

### Project 008 — Independent Wireless Earbud Audio Routing and Multi-Device Communication System
**Class:** PERSONAL-FIRST SYSTEM

An investigation into whether left and right wireless earpieces can operate with greater independence than conventional paired playback, such as receiving different sources, devices, channels, or communication roles. Feasibility is likely constrained by Bluetooth generations/profiles, LE Audio, operating-system audio routing, firmware, chipset behavior, synchronization, latency, radio architecture, and hardware design. No implementation commitment should occur before these constraints are established.

**Status:** TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY MUST BE ESTABLISHED FIRST.

### Project 009 — Interactive AI Virtual Audience for Presentation and Public-Speaking Rehearsal
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM — provisional; may merge into P001

A simulated audience environment for practicing speeches, presentations, pitches, lectures, interviews, and other live communication. Configurable audience members could ask questions, challenge claims, represent different expertise levels or attitudes, and potentially provide feedback. It overlaps Project 001 substantially; research must determine whether it has an independently defensible problem/architecture, should become a broader layer around Project 001, or should ultimately merge with it.

**Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED; RELATIONSHIP TO PROJECT 001 UNRESOLVED.

### Project 010 — Fantasy Action RPG Inspired by Ensemble Adventure Animation
**Class:** CREATIVE / ENTERTAINMENT PROJECT

An original game concept inspired at a high level by the appeal of ensemble fantasy adventures: a party of distinctive characters, complementary abilities, fantasy combat, exploration, narrative progression, and team dynamics. The project must not copy protected characters, storylines, dialogue, artwork, locations, or other protected expressive elements. Research should verify licensed games and adjacent fantasy party/action RPGs before making market or novelty claims, then identify an original gameplay and narrative identity.

**Status:** CONCEPT / RESEARCH STAGE.

### Project 011 — Geopolitical Risk to Economic Impact Forecasting and Warning System
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

A system that monitors national and global political events such as tariffs, sanctions, elections, export restrictions, diplomatic breakdowns, and conflict, then estimates and communicates plausible downstream economic effects for a selected country or market. Candidate outputs include warnings about price pressure, shortages, trade disruption, commodity exposure, exchange-rate pressure, or sector-specific risk. Research must distinguish causal evidence, scenario analysis, nowcasting, forecasting, event extraction, geopolitical-risk indices, and speculative narrative generation.

**Status:** HIGH-INTEREST RESEARCH CANDIDATE; PRIOR ART, CAUSAL VALIDITY, DATA, AND FORECASTING EVALUATION REQUIRED.

### Project 012 — Internet Compression and Optimization Proxy System
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

An application-level proxy intended to reduce transferred data and improve perceived responsiveness in bandwidth-constrained environments through safe, measurable optimization techniques. Research must establish what is technically possible under modern HTTPS/TLS, HTTP/2, HTTP/3/QUIC, content encodings, CDNs, browser security, image/media compression, caching, and end-to-end encryption. Performance claims must be measured rather than assumed.

**Status:** STRONG SYSTEMS RESEARCH CANDIDATE; MODERN PROTOCOL CONSTRAINTS MAY FORCE REFRAME.

### Project 013 — StartupMatch: Founder–Investor Matching Platform
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

A two-sided marketplace that connects startup founders with potentially compatible investors through structured profiles, matching logic, secure communication, transparency controls, and real-time interaction. Research must distinguish ordinary marketplace filtering from defensible matching/recommendation methods and must address trust, fraud, privacy, identity, ranking bias, cold-start behavior, security, and marketplace incentives.

**Status:** PRODUCT-VIABLE CONCEPT; ALGORITHMIC DIFFERENTIATION AND MARKETPLACE PRIOR ART REQUIRE RESEARCH.

### Project 014 — Automated Log Analysis and Root-Cause Assistance Tool
**Class:** GENERAL / PRODUCT SYSTEM

A lightweight utility that parses heterogeneous or unstructured logs, extracts anomalous events, correlates evidence across time or sources, and assists operators in identifying plausible root causes. Research must separate deterministic parsing, anomaly detection, event correlation, causal/root-cause inference, retrieval, and LLM-generated explanations. Evaluation should use known incident ground truth where possible rather than judging fluent explanations alone.

**Status:** STRONG ENGINEERING/FYP CANDIDATE; OBSERVABILITY AND AIOPS PRIOR ART IS EXPECTED TO BE CROWDED.

Personal-mastery projects may proceed independently of the FYP/product ranking.

## Portfolio-wide research protocol
Where applicable, each project proceeds through: discovery → genuine historical lineage → academic literature → current state of practice → commercial systems → open-source implementations → patents/standards/regulation where relevant → competing and failed approaches → prior-art matrix → gap analysis → novelty threats → problem definition → evidence-backed differentiation → feasibility → security/privacy/ethics → architecture candidates → rejected alternatives → go/park/kill recommendation → implementation.

Historical research goes back only as far as the real intellectual or technological lineage warrants; it is not artificially forced into a century-by-century narrative.
