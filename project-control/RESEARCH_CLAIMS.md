Important claims must have evidence and an explicit status before documentation treats them as findings.

| C-004 | Codex can consume and maintain the shared project-control protocol end-to-end | Workflow capability | None yet | UNSUPPORTED | Requires controlled handoff test |

## Claim Status Meanings

- VERIFIED — directly demonstrated by adequate evidence.
- SUPPORTED — evidence supports the claim but does not fully establish it.
- PARTIAL — only part of the claim has evidence.
- UNSUPPORTED — no adequate evidence yet.
- CONTRADICTED — evidence conflicts with the claim.
- SUPERSEDED — replaced by newer evidence/claim.
- UNDER REVIEW — evidence is being evaluated.

## Rule
If evidence does not justify a claim, downgrade the wording rather than strengthening the narrative.
