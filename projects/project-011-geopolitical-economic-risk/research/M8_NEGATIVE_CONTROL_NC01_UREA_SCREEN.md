# P003 M8 — Negative-Control Candidate NC-01 Screening

## Candidate
2022 Russia/Ukraine fertilizer shock → **Nigeria urea-import warning** (HS 310210).

## Status
**PROMISING NEGATIVE-CONTROL CANDIDATE; NOT YET ADMITTED.**

## Why this candidate is useful
The 2022 geopolitical shock created an obvious global fertilizer narrative. Russia was a major global fertilizer exporter, so an ungated system could plausibly generalize the shock into a Nigerian fertilizer/urea warning.

But the pre-event Nigerian trade structure for the predeclared target **urea (HS 310210)** looks very different from Nigeria's broader fertilizer exposure.

## Pre-event exposure contrast
Current WITS/UN Comtrade reconstruction for 2021 reports:
- all fertilizer HS 31 imports by Nigeria: about USD 206.585m;
- imports from Russia across HS 31: about USD 41.324m;
- HS 310430 potassium sulphate: about USD 41.137m total, of which about USD 41.113m came from Russia;
- HS 310210 urea: only about USD 0.162m total, reported mainly from the Netherlands, with small India/Turkey values and no Russia entry in the displayed partner set.

This means the broad statement "Nigeria imported fertilizer from Russia" is true, while the narrower statement "Nigeria depended on Russian urea imports" is not supported by this 2021 reporter record.

## Why this is not yet a valid negative control
The frozen negative-control rule requires more than current retrospective absence in one trade table.

Before admission we must establish:
1. exact event/cutoff relevant to the fertilizer shock;
2. cutoff-safe or clearly labeled R2 pre-event trade evidence;
3. whether mirror data reveal material Russian urea shipments omitted from Nigeria's reporter record;
4. whether indirect/re-export channels materially change the exposure conclusion;
5. that the target was frozen before B2/B3 outputs;
6. independent evidence that the chosen urea pathway was negligible/absent, not merely unreported.

## Important specificity lesson
A control at **HS 31 fertilizer** would fail because Nigeria had material Russian fertilizer exposure, concentrated in potassium sulphate.
The possible control is specifically **HS 310210 urea**.

Therefore:
global event → broad sector label is insufficient.
Product-level typing changes the exposure conclusion.

## Expected behavior if admitted
A baseline may discuss the global fertilizer shock.
It should not release a material Nigeria-specific **Russian-urea-import** warning unless another admissible transmission path is established.

## Current decision
NC-01: HOLD / PROMISING.
Do not score B2 or B3 yet.
Do not use later Nigerian fertilizer outcomes to decide whether the control is valid.
