# P003 M8 — Candidate C T3 Processing/Transmission Pass

## Status
**T3 MECHANISM PARTIALLY SUPPORTED; NOT YET RELEASED AS A CLEAN POLICY-PRICE TRANSMISSION EDGE.**

## Narrow chain
October-2023 FX-access change
→ legally importable crude palm oil (HS 151110)
→ Nigerian palm-oil supply/industrial utilization
→ food/manufacturing uses
→ possible downstream supply/cost effects.

## Independent mechanism evidence
USDA/FAS Nigeria Oilseeds and Products Annual (2023) states that Nigeria relies on palm-oil imports to bridge a domestic supply gap and that private investors are expanding processing capacity.

USDA/FAS (2024) describes palm oil as both a household cooking ingredient and a significant raw material used in noodles, soaps, detergents and cosmetics. It also reports expanding processing capacity and continued substantial palm-oil consumption.

These sources support the existence of a real domestic utilization/processing channel rather than a purely semantic HS-to-retail mapping.

## Strong counterevidence / mechanism modifiers
The 2024 FAS report also records:
- continuing difficulty obtaining foreign currency after the October 2023 restriction removal;
- substantial domestic production;
- increased domestic processing/production investment;
- unrecorded cross-border/gray-channel palm-oil flows;
- industry contacts reporting that imports were not fully impeded even while the item was on the restricted FX list.

Therefore the policy change cannot be treated as an on/off switch for palm-oil availability.

## T3 judgment
Supported components:
1. Nigeria has actual palm-oil import dependence/supply-gap exposure.
2. Palm oil has documented domestic household and industrial uses.
3. Domestic processing capacity exists and is expanding.

Unresolved components:
1. how much HS 151110 specifically entered domestic refining versus other uses;
2. whether official FX access materially changed landed cost/quantity after 12-Oct-2023;
3. magnitude and timing of pass-through;
4. mapping from crude palm oil to the NBS bottled-vegetable-oil item.

## Decision
T1: VERIFIED.
T2: SUPPORTED retrospectively/R2 for HS 151110.
T3: CONDITIONAL / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED in research terms; under the current conservative engine, CONDITIONAL does not pass.
T4+: WITHHELD.

This is a useful test of the framework: real processing/use evidence exists, but it is still insufficient to release a clean downstream consumer-price scenario.
