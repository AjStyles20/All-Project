# AJ Project Portfolio — Project Catalogue

## Purpose
Canonical short-form registry after the 10 September 2026 renumbering and the completed 16 September 2026 Final Portfolio Research Gate. Historical folders retain previous IDs; use `CANONICAL_PROJECT_INDEX.md` as identity/documentation authority.

## Portfolio rule
No project is to be called unique, first-of-its-kind, unprecedented, secure, production-ready, or otherwise stronger than available evidence supports. Absence from search results is not evidence of non-existence. Implementation does not mean verification, and Drive presence does not mean audit/freeze. Broader historical contribution claims superseded by the Final Portfolio Gate remain preserved as research lineage rather than silently rewritten.
