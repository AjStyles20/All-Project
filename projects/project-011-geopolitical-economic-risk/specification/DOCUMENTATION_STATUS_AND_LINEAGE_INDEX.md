## Why older files disagree with current status

The repository intentionally preserves the research chronology. Early research passes contain statements such as `Implementation authorization: NO`, `PARK`, or `READY TO PARK`. Those statements describe the decision state **at the time each pass was written**. They are not current instructions.

Do not edit those historical passes merely to make their wording match the present state. Their value is that they show how the broad idea was attacked, narrowed, and only later authorized.

## Authority order for current status

When two documents appear to conflict, interpret them in this order:

1. portfolio governance decisions, especially DR-010;
2. current P003 root `README.md`;
3. `IMPLEMENTATION_ENTRY_CONTRACT.md`;
4. current milestone/readiness audits;
5. frozen experiment manifests/contracts;
6. historical formal research specification;
7. PASS_001–PASS_005 research records.

A later authority may supersede an earlier status decision without erasing the earlier research evidence.

## Current scientific boundary

The contribution under test is:

**typed transmission-edge evidence sufficiency + weakest-link downstream claim progression.**

ETEC — Economic Transmission Evidence Contract — is the architecture used to operationalize that mechanism. Generic evidence contracts, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), provenance, knowledge graphs, contradiction handling and abstention are not claimed as the contribution by themselves.

## Current evidence boundary

Software tests and deterministic fixtures establish implementation behavior only.

They do **not** establish:
- empirical B3 superiority over B2;
- lower real Unsupported Downstream Claim Rate (UDCR);
- acceptable inter-rater reliability;
- validated forecast accuracy;
- causal forecasting ability;
- completion of M9 or M10.

The six first-wave B2 model runs remain unexecuted, and no independent human reviewer judgments have been collected.
