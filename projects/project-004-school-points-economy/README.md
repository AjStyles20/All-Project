# Project 004 — Gamified School Achievement, Rewards and Digital Economy System

## Concept
A governed school platform in which selected academic, extracurricular, behavioral, or institutional achievements can generate points/rewards that students track and potentially use within institution-defined rules.

## Current stage
RESEARCH FOUNDATION — problem and incentive model are not yet validated.

## Research mandate
Investigate historical and current educational reward/token economies, gamification, motivation and incentive effects, school behavior/reward systems, digital credential/reward products, fairness and accessibility, abuse/fraud resistance, privacy, governance, unintended incentives, and whether a digital economy solves a meaningful educational/institutional problem.

## Boundary
Do not assume that adding points improves motivation or learning. Positive, negative, null, and context-dependent evidence must all be retained.
