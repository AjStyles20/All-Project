# P002 Research Pass 013 — Adversarial Prior-Art Attack on the Expanded Learning World

## Status

## Purpose
Passes 011–012 expanded P002 from an AI virtual classroom into a personal learning world that can optionally become a hybrid human/AI learning society. This pass begins cutting that expanded concept back down by searching for prior art around six newer hypotheses:

1. Knowledge Issue Tracker.
2. Learning Near-Miss Detector.
3. Human/AI Substitutable Learning Guilds.
4. Policy-Semantic Virtual Campus.
5. Evidence-Governed Community Knowledge Forge.
6. Personal Learning Operating World.

The test is not whether an exact product name exists. The test is whether the underlying mechanism is already substantially represented in education, HCI, CSCL, personal learning environments, knowledge building, or human-AI collaboration.

## Search evidence — first adversarial sweep

### A. Community knowledge and epistemic authority are established research directions
A 2026 community-based AI learning framework explicitly argues that AI should not automatically hold epistemic authority. It proposes redistribution of authority, situated discernment, and foregrounding community knowledge. This directly weakens any novelty claim based only on separating AI knowledge from community knowledge.

Knowledge-building theory is much older and already treats learning as collaborative advancement of community knowledge, continual idea improvement, public dialogue, constructive use of authoritative information, and production of epistemic artifacts. A 2026 Knowledge Forum study specifically targets collaborative inquiry, process documentation, reflective iteration, and artifact creation.

Conclusion: **Community Knowledge Forge is NOT novel at the level of collaborative knowledge construction.** Any surviving contribution must concern enforceable provenance/authority transitions, AI-human mixed authorship, contradiction/dependency propagation, or another mechanism not already covered by knowledge-building environments.

### B. Human/AI role variation and hybrid collaboration are established
Prior human-AI teamwork research defines multiple functional AI teammate roles. Recent educational work treats AI as tool, proactive assistant, co-learner, peer collaborator, or social/cognitive teammate. A 2026 human-human-AI programming study reports that adding a human partner alongside AI can increase social presence and reduce reliance on AI-generated code relative to human-AI dyads.

A 2026 Computers & Education study models Human-GenAI shared agency and identifies different human/AI collaboration profiles within a learning community. Recent work also experimentally compares supportive and contrarian AI personas in group learning.

Conclusion: **'AI can occupy different group roles' and 'mixed human/AI groups' are NOT novel.**

Surviving question: can a learning activity define role contracts independently of occupant type, allow a role to be occupied by a human or AI under explicit capability/trust constraints, and evaluate what happens as team composition changes? This remains **UNKNOWN**, not novel.

### C. Personal Learning Environments are established
Personal Learning Environment (PLE) literature already defines learner-centred ecosystems of tools/resources configured around individual educational goals. Current literature explicitly examines generative AI inside PLEs, personalization, autonomy and self-regulation.

Conclusion: **Personal Learning Operating World is NOT novel merely because it is personal, persistent, customizable, or AI-assisted.**

Surviving possibility: integration of personal knowledge issues, provenance, learner-state evidence, social/AI role substitution, and policy-semantic spaces into a coherent personal environment may be useful, but integration alone is not assumed novel.

### D. Policy-semantic virtual spaces remain under-defined
The search found abundant virtual/metaverse learning environments, but this first sweep did not establish a system where entering a spatial location itself activates a formally inspectable bundle of epistemic authority rules, AI permissions, privacy boundaries, assistance policy, assessment policy and provenance requirements.

This is **UNKNOWN**, not evidence of absence.

Important distinction:
- cosmetic/immersive room = prior art;
- room as policy/security/pedagogical execution context = candidate mechanism requiring deeper search.

The virtual-world interface must still justify itself against ordinary navigation. If spatial representation does not improve rule comprehension, social presence, task switching, memory, or engagement enough to justify complexity, it should be removed.

### E. Knowledge Issue Tracker is adjacent to established knowledge-building and learner-model work
Knowledge Forum and related knowledge-building systems already support persistent ideas, questions, discourse, revision and community knowledge advancement. Intelligent tutoring systems also maintain misconceptions/learner states.

Therefore a simple persistent list of 'things I do not understand' is unlikely to be novel.

The stronger candidate is narrower:

> A persistent, evidence-bearing learning issue that cannot be closed merely because an explanation was delivered; closure requires learner-produced evidence of understanding, and the issue retains attempted explanations, source evidence, misconceptions, assistance history and later relapse/reopening.

Novelty state: **UNKNOWN/PARTIAL**. This must be compared directly against mastery-learning systems, knowledge tracing, issue-based learning, question-management tools, misconception tracking and mastery verification.

### F. Learning Near-Miss Detector remains promising as an integration hypothesis, not a new educational principle
Education has long distinguished correct answers from understanding; misconception diagnosis, confidence judgments, process data, knowledge tracing and reasoning assessment all attack this problem. Therefore 'correct answer does not imply understanding' is obviously NOT novel.

The candidate mechanism is a structured near-miss event that detects suspicious success patterns such as:
- correct final answer + contradictory reasoning;
- correct answer + unsupported evidence;
- correct answer after high assistance escalation;
- correct answer + very low confidence;
- correct answer produced by copying/AI dependence without later independent success;
- correct outcome while underlying misconception remains active.

The event would schedule targeted re-verification rather than immediately granting mastery.

Novelty state: **UNKNOWN/PARTIAL**. Likely useful even if not novel.

## Important new threat: social AI should augment, not erase human collaboration
A 2026 scoping review of GenAI and student collaboration emphasizes active human participation and warns against substituting critical thinking and interaction. A 2026 human-human-AI programming study found that triadic collaboration reduced reliance on AI-generated code relative to a human-AI dyad, especially where AI use became visible/accountable to a human peer.

This materially changes P002's social philosophy:

**Private World:** AI may simulate missing social/pedagogical roles because no human peers are present.

**Hybrid/Social World:** AI should generally augment available human relationships rather than unnecessarily replace them.

Therefore the system should be capable of graceful role handoff: an AI role can yield to a human participant, become facilitator/evidence checker, or disappear when its pedagogical function is no longer justified.

## New architecture hypothesis — Role Contract + Occupant
Instead of defining permanent characters first, define a pedagogical/social role contract:

RoleContract
- purpose
- allowed actions
- prohibited actions
- evidence authority
- private-data scope
- intervention conditions
- communication rules
- expected learning contribution
- evaluation metrics

Occupant
- HUMAN
- AI_AGENT
- HYBRID/ASSISTED HUMAN (future research only)

A role might be `SKEPTIC`, `EXPLAINER`, `FACILITATOR`, `EVIDENCE_CHECKER`, `NOVICE_PEER`, `MENTOR`, `ASSESSOR`, or `PROJECT_PARTNER`.

The research question becomes not 'can AI pretend to be a classmate?' but:

> Under what conditions can humans and AI occupy bounded learning roles, how should authority and privacy change with occupant type, and when should an AI role yield to a human participant?

This is a stronger scientific question, but novelty remains **UNKNOWN**.

## Community knowledge model — revised
Do not treat community consensus as truth.

Candidate layers:
1. Canonical source knowledge — approved/authoritative materials.
2. Verified derived knowledge — claims with evidence/derivation.
3. Community artifacts — notes, explanations, examples, debates and projects.
4. Personal knowledge state — learner understanding, misconceptions, unresolved issues.
5. Conversational/social content — lowest authority by default.

Promotion between layers must be explicit. Popularity must never automatically promote authority.

This aligns with emerging 2026 work arguing that epistemic authority in AI-mediated education should be negotiated rather than automatically assigned to AI, while adding a systems-engineering question: can those authority distinctions be made machine-enforceable and auditable?

## Possible product identity emerging
The product and research contribution may need separate descriptions.

### Product vision
A personal learning world that can grow into a human-AI learning society: private when the learner wants solitude, social when humans join, and supported by AI roles that adapt rather than replace people by default.

This is NOT an approved novelty statement.
