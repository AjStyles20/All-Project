# P003 — M8 Historical Case-Set Registry v1

## Status
**FROZEN BEFORE REAL B2 EXECUTION OR HUMAN OUTCOME JUDGMENT.**

This registry closes opportunistic case acquisition for the current M8/M9 evaluation design. New cases may be researched later, but they must be versioned as prospective extensions and may not be retroactively inserted into this registry.

## Research mechanism under test
P003 does not test whether a language model can write plausible geopolitical-economic narratives. It tests **typed transmission-edge evidence sufficiency and weakest-link downstream claim progression**: a downstream Nigerian claim may progress only when each required economic edge has sufficient evidence; missing, conditional, contradicted or stale links must constrain or stop progression.

## Why these cases are sufficient for the bounded experiment
The set exercises materially different pathway states rather than maximizing case count:
- successful bounded progression through T4 in two different economic mechanisms;
- a narrow negative control that should not release exposure;
- a missing-exposure stop;
- a conditional domestic-transmission stop.

This is enough to test the software/protocol behavior across progression and abstention states. It is **not** enough to claim universal geopolitical forecasting validity or population-level statistical generalization.

## Anti-selection rules
From this freeze onward:
1. do not add a case because it produces favourable B3 behavior;
2. do not drop a case because B2 performs well or B3 performs poorly;
3. do not alter transition states to increase coverage;
4. do not convert unknown/insufficient exposure into a negative control;
5. do not use later outcomes to repair missing historical edges;
6. any new case is a new registry version/prospective extension.

## Interpretation boundary
Possible future evidence can support or falsify the bounded contribution. It cannot establish:
- universal causal inference;
- universal Nigerian economic forecasting;
- numerical forecast calibration without T5/T6;
- scientific superiority from deterministic fixtures alone;
- B3-vs-B2 superiority before real baseline outputs and independent judgments.

## M8 closure criterion
