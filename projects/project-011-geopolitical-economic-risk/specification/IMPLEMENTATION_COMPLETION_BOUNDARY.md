1. repairs a correctness or security defect;
2. is necessary to execute or preserve the frozen external experiment;
3. is necessary to ingest real external evidence without changing its meaning; or
4. is an explicitly versioned prospective extension that cannot affect the frozen first-wave evaluation.

Otherwise the correct action is to preserve the implementation and collect the external evidence.
