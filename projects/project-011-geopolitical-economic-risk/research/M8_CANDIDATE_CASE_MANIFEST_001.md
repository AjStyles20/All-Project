# P003 M8 — Candidate Case Manifest 001

## Purpose

This is a **candidate-screening document**, not an admitted historical experiment and not a result.

The first screening compares two mechanism families against the frozen M8 admission rules.

## Candidate A — 2022 Russia–Ukraine shock / selected food-trade exposure

**Event anchor:** Russian military operations/invasion of Ukraine beginning 24 February 2022.

Authoritative event anchors:
- UN Secretary-General statement, 24 February 2022: https://press.un.org/en/2022/sgsm21158.doc.htm
- UN General Assembly resolution coverage, 2 March 2022: https://press.un.org/en/2022/ga12407.doc.htm

### Current admission status: HOLD — NEEDS PRE-CUTOFF EXPOSURE RECONSTRUCTION

Strengths:
- event date/scope can be anchored to UN records;
- commodity/product exposure can in principle be represented with HS-coded trade data;
- Nigeria-specific trade observations are available in Comtrade-derived datasets;
- later Nigerian CPI/macro outcomes can be sourced independently from NBS/CBN.

Unresolved admission questions:
1. Which exact product family is declared before outcome inspection?
2. What information cutoff is scientifically defensible: event date, first post-event assessment date, or another predeclared horizon?
3. Was the relevant Nigeria exposure dataset actually available by that cutoff, rather than merely reconstructable later?
4. What explicit product→sector/use→COICOP concordance supports any consumer-price claim?
5. What competing mechanisms/counterevidence (domestic production, substitute suppliers, inventories, exchange rate, policy response) were knowable at the cutoff?
6. What local target is fixed before scoring?

Do not admit this case until these questions are resolved.

## Candidate B — Red Sea commercial-shipping disruption beginning November 2023

**Event anchor:** attacks against international shipping in the Red Sea area beginning in November 2023.

Authoritative anchors:
- IMO statement, 19 December 2023: https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/secretarygeneral/pages/red-sea-statement-19-december-2023.aspx
- IMO Security Council statement, 3 January 2024: https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/secretarygeneral/pages/maintenance-of-international-peace-and-security-%E2%80%93-united-nations-security-council%2C-9525th-meeting.aspx
- IMO incident update, 8 May 2024: https://wwwcdn.imo.org/localresources/en/MediaCentre/HotTopics/Documents/Red%20Sea/Red%20Sea%20Incidents%20-%208%20May%202024.pdf

### Current admission status: HOLD — ROUTE-TO-NIGERIA EXPOSURE LINK NOT YET ESTABLISHED

Strengths:
- event and route disruption are strongly documented by IMO;
- IMO documented commercial rerouting and longer voyages;
- mechanism differs from direct product-origin shock, improving potential mechanism diversity.

Unresolved admission questions:
1. Which Nigeria-bound product/route exposure was measurably affected at the chosen cutoff?
2. Can route exposure be established without inferring that all Nigerian imports experienced the same disruption?
3. Is there an authoritative freight/shipping series available at the historical cutoff?
4. What local outcome is predeclared and independently measurable?
5. Can shipping-cost transmission be separated from exchange-rate, fuel-price and domestic logistics effects?

Until those links are documented, the case cannot progress from global event evidence to a Nigerian local-warning claim.

## Candidate negative control — NOT YET SELECTED

The negative control must be selected by a rule independent of observed B3 performance. It should have:
- high geopolitical/news salience;
- an objectively testable event;
- negligible or absent Nigeria exposure for the declared product/route;
- sufficient data to demonstrate the absence/weakness of exposure;
- no selection based on whether ETEC happens to abstain.

## Data-source qualification retained

UN Comtrade query dimensions include classification, commodity code, period, reporter, partner and trade flow. Because Comtrade updates records as official information becomes available, historical replay must preserve retrieval/vintage metadata and distinguish information available at the cutoff from later reconstruction.

CBN statistics can provide macro/external-sector context, but macro co-movement is not automatically a T3 causal transmission edge.

NBS CPI can provide later outcome evidence where the declared target is a consumer-price category. CPI expenditure weights must not be used as pass-through coefficients.

## Decision

**No historical case admitted in this pass.**

This is a deliberate result of the evidence gate, not a project failure. The next pass must resolve Candidate A's product/cutoff/exposure/concordance questions and Candidate B's route-to-Nigeria exposure question before either can enter the frozen evaluation set.
