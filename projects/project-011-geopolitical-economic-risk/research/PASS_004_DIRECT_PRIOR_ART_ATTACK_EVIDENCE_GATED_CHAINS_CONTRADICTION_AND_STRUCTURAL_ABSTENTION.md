## 1. Research question
Does prior art already implement the surviving P003 composition from Pass 003: provenance-bearing event→exposure→mechanism→local-impact pathways, explicit counterevidence/contradiction handling, and structural abstention when required economic links are unsupported?

## 2. Executive falsification result
The broad composition is under severe prior-art pressure. Recent work already demonstrates: knowledge-graph supply-chain risk propagation; explainable multi-hop risk-conduction paths; causal/temporal risk graphs; evidence-gated graph construction; preservation of contradictory claims with provenance; calibrated abstention; and graph coverage/knowledge-boundary disclosure.

Therefore P003 must NOT claim novelty for any of the following in isolation or generic combination:
- causal/risk knowledge graphs;
- multi-hop explainable risk propagation;
- provenance-bearing evidence graphs;
- evidence-gated graph construction;
- contradiction preservation;
- uncertainty-aware or calibrated abstention;
- counterfactual/mitigation reasoning;
- LLM + KG geopolitical/supply-chain reasoning.

The remaining candidate is domain- and task-specific: **an operational contract for converting a verified geopolitical event into a target-country/local economic-impact claim, where each economic transmission edge has explicit admissibility requirements, counterevidence can demote already-supported edges, and output granularity is mechanically capped at the weakest required link.** This is still a hypothesis, not a verified novelty claim.

## 3. Strongest direct threats
### 3.1 Industrial-chain dynamic KG risk monitoring (2026)
Guo et al. propose UCGF, aligning dynamic behavioral anomaly graphs with static knowledge-graph semantics and using neuro-symbolic multi-hop propagation to produce interpretable risk-conduction paths. This directly kills any claim that explainable graph-based propagation itself is new.

### 3.2 Supply-chain KG reasoning lineage
Kosasih et al. (2022) combine graph neural networks and KG reasoning to infer hidden supply-chain relationship risks. Yang et al. (2024) construct a large supply-chain KG for risk monitoring, early warning and decision support. Other work uses uncertain KGs, confidence scores, macroeconomic graph alignment and LLM extraction. P003 cannot claim supply-network graph reasoning, uncertainty-bearing links, or external-macroeconomic graph enrichment as new.

### 3.3 Evidence-gated, contradiction-preserving KGs outside economics
Recent AI-security KG research explicitly accepts candidate relations only when verified against source sentences and preserves contradictory claims with provenance rather than collapsing them. This is a direct conceptual threat to the phrase `evidence-gated graph with contradiction preservation` as a contribution.

### 3.4 Causal-action and temporal/counterfactual risk graphs
2026 risk-management work encodes directional causal chains, mitigation actions, temporal dependencies and counterfactual interventions in KGs. Hence `causal graph + mitigation` and `risk graph + counterevidence/counterfactual` are not novel abstractions.

### 3.5 Calibrated abstention plus evidence graphs
Recent accountable knowledge-augmented decision frameworks combine time-indexed evidence graphs, explicit defeaters/conflict resolution and calibrated risk gates that abstain to a human. Therefore generic `evidence + contradiction + abstention` is not a defensible novelty claim.

### 3.6 Coverage disclosure / knowledge-boundary detection
Recent KG/LLM systems explicitly discuss disclosure when a query falls outside graph coverage, and recent supply-chain world-model research discusses knowledge-boundary detection. Therefore `tell the user when knowledge is missing` is also established.

## 5. Narrowed contribution hypothesis
### Economic Transmission Evidence Contract (ETEC) — working name
For a target country and verified geopolitical event, define an explicit evidence contract for each transition class:

`EVENT -> EXPOSURE -> DOMESTIC TRANSMISSION -> LOCAL OUTCOME -> MAGNITUDE`

Each transition class declares:
1. admissible evidence types;
2. minimum source/provenance requirements;
3. applicability period and geography;
4. required economic identifiers/classifications;
5. counterevidence/defeaters;
6. staleness rules;
7. allowed output class if the edge passes;
8. mandatory abstention/downgrade behavior if it fails.

The system's maximum claim strength is the strongest output class supported by **all required preceding edges**, not the confidence or fluency of a generative model.

Example:
- official export restriction: VERIFIED;
- target-country import exposure by HS code: VERIFIED;
- domestic input-use mapping: SUPPORTED;
- pass-through magnitude model: INSUFFICIENT;

Allowed output: `mechanism-supported directional scenario`.
Forbidden output: numeric consumer-price forecast.

## 6. Counterevidence semantics
Counterevidence must operate on graph state, not merely appear in explanatory prose.

Candidate transitions:
- VERIFIED -> CONTESTED when authoritative sources conflict;
- SUPPORTED -> WEAKENED when diversification/substitution evidence is material;
- ACTIVE -> STALE when validity horizon expires;
- EXPOSURE -> LOW/INSUFFICIENT when newer trade data invalidates dependency;
- MODEL-ELIGIBLE -> SCENARIO-ONLY when model applicability conditions fail.

A warning is therefore revisable and defeasible. P003 must preserve the reason for every promotion and demotion.

## 7. Structural abstention becomes domain contract enforcement
Pass 003 used `structural abstention`. Pass 004 narrows that term because generic structural/coverage abstention is already threatened.

P003's potentially defensible behavior is **economic-contract abstention**:
- missing HS/product mapping -> cannot establish trade exposure;
- missing target-country exposure -> cannot claim localized impact;
- missing input-output/use relationship -> cannot cross from imported product to domestic sector;
- missing consumer mapping -> cannot cross from sector to household category;
- invalid/out-of-domain econometric model -> cannot issue magnitude;
- material unresolved counterevidence -> claim remains contested or downgraded.

This is deterministic policy over evidence state where possible. An LLM may help extract/categorize candidate evidence, but it does not decide that missing evidence exists merely from self-confidence.

## 8. Critical novelty boundary
The project must not defend itself by saying the individual technologies already exist but `our combination is unique`. Combination novelty requires evidence that the integration creates a materially different mechanism and measurable behavior.

The future experiment therefore has to show that ETEC produces useful differences against credible baselines, such as:
- fewer unsupported downstream claims;
- better expert agreement on claim strength;
- correct downgrading after counterevidence;
- better provenance completeness;
- fewer false numeric forecasts;
- acceptable warning coverage despite abstention.

If it merely produces longer explanations around the same predictions, the contribution fails.

## 9. Security / adversarial implication
Evidence contracts increase robustness only if evidence itself is trustworthy. Required later threat model:
- source impersonation and fake official notices;
- syndicated articles counted as independent corroboration;
- stale/retracted data;
- prompt injection in retrieved documents;
- entity/HS-code mis-resolution;
- poisoned trade or graph data;
- forged timestamps;
- counterevidence suppression;
- LLM-generated unsupported bridge edges.

Server-side provenance and source policy must be authoritative. Retrieved content is untrusted input.

## 10. Kill criteria after this pass
Park or reframe ETEC if a directly comparable geopolitical/economic system is found that already implements substantially all of:
1. event-to-target-country economic transmission edge classes;
2. edge-specific admissibility rules tied to economic data types;
3. provenance and temporal applicability per edge;
4. counterevidence that mechanically demotes pathway state;
5. output granularity capped by weakest/missing required edge;
6. explicit abstention at the broken economic link;
7. empirical comparison showing reduced unsupported economic claims.

Also park if available Nigerian data cannot support repeatable edge adjudication at useful resolution.
